# University Assignment Research Guidelines

For all university coursework requiring academic rigor: sourcing, citation, and documentation.

## Before Starting an Assignment

1. **Identify key claims** - What specific numbers, processes, or facts need academic support?
2. **Source systematically** - Don't rely on initial estimates. Verify against peer-reviewed research
3. **Search strategy** - Use: Google Scholar, MDPI, ScienceDirect, journal databases, ResearchGate
4. **Preferred sources** - Peer-reviewed journals > conference papers > institutional reports > web

## As You Write

- **Cite as you go** - Add in-text citations while writing, not after
- **Build bibliography simultaneously** - Keep reference list synced with citations
- **Use consistent format** - APA for most coursework (author, year, doi)
- **Track sources with links** - Save DOI or URL for every source used

## When Submitting

- **Create AI disclosure** - If you used AI assistance, include a brief document explaining what AI helped with and what was your original work
- **Proofread citations** - Verify all in-text citations match bibliography entries
- **Include bibliography** - Full reference list with proper formatting at end of assignment

## Example Workflow

**Assignment:** Life Cycle Assessment of dairy vs. soy milk

**Process:**
1. Write initial draft with best estimates and general knowledge
2. Identify main claims needing sources: water usage, processing steps, environmental impacts
3. Search peer-reviewed databases for LCA studies on milk production
4. Compare your numbers against published data (verify or correct)
5. Add in-text citations to all major claims: (Author, Year)
6. Build bibliography in APA format with DOI links
7. Create AI disclosure if applicable
8. Final review: citations match, no orphaned claims

## Citation Format (APA)

**In-text:** (Poore & Nemecek, 2018) or As Poore and Nemecek (2018) found...

**Bibliography:**
```
Poore, J., & Nemecek, T. (2018). Reducing food's environmental impacts through 
producers and consumers. Science, 360(6392), 987-992. 
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaq0216
```

## Red Flags

- Specific numbers without sources (water usage, emissions data, processing times)
- Claims about environmental impacts without peer-reviewed support
- Assumptions presented as facts
- No bibliography section

## Resources

- **Google Scholar:** scholar.google.com (free access to many papers)
- **MDPI:** mdpi.com (open access peer-reviewed journals)
- **ResearchGate:** researchgate.net (researchers share papers)
- **Your university library:** Often provides access to paywalled journals
- **Zotero:** Free reference management software
