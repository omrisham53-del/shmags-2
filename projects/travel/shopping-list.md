# Norway Trekking Shopping List

Built 2026-08-31 from the unchecked items in `tracker.md`. Departure Sept 8, so 8 days.

Tick items here as they're bought, then sync back into `tracker.md`.

## Priority 1: trip-blocking

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| [ ] Sleeping bag liner (lakenpose) | Mandatory at every DNT cabin, all 11 nights. Silk or synthetic, mummy shape. No liner, no bed. |
| [ ] Hiking shoes | Salomon per the family advice, waterproof, below-ankle. Try on loaded. If ankle feels unstable, go mid-cut: Besseggen + boulder fields with a multi-day pack is the real test. |
| [ ] Waterproof shell jacket | Only skip if the existing rain jacket is confirmed a real hardshell. Check the label before shopping. |
| [ ] Dry bags, 2-3 sizes | One large pack liner, smaller ones for sleeping bag and electronics. |
| [ ] Power bank, 20,000mAh | Old phone battery, and it backs up the map plus emergency comms. |
| [ ] Paper map + compass | Norge-serien 1:50,000, Jotunheimen and Breheimen sheets. If unavailable in Israel, buy maps at the DNT shop in Oslo on Sep 9. Compass buy locally. |
| [ ] Gas stove | Fuel is a Norway purchase. Confirm the valve is standard screw-thread (EN 417) so Oslo canisters fit. |
| [ ] Water carry, 2L minimum | Bottle plus bladder, or two bottles. |

## Food to bring from Israel

Norway is expensive, so dry goods come from home. Perishables (cream cheese, smoked salmon, salami) bought there.

- [ ] Ptitim or orzo, portioned per meal
- [ ] Soup or sauce sachets
- [ ] Crackers
- [ ] Tahini and halva
- [ ] Hard cheese
- [ ] Dried dates and apricots
- [ ] Trail mix
- [ ] Protein and energy bars
- [ ] Spices in small containers
- [ ] Minimal cooking tools (pot, spork, lighter)

Volume: Jotunheimen days 1-4 are staffed huts with meals, so mainly lunches needed there. Breheimen is 4 consecutive self-service nights, so carry full breakfast, lunch and dinner for those. Confirm at the Sep 9 DNT visit whether the Breheimen self-service cabins stock a provision store, which would cut that weight.

## Do not buy in Israel

- Gas canister: banned on flights, buy in Oslo Sep 9 (XXL or the DNT shop)
- DNT key: collected in Oslo, requires active membership

## Admin, not shopping

- [ ] DNT membership (~EUR 60) purchased online before Sep 9, the key depends on it
- [ ] Travel insurance explicitly covering multi-day mountain trekking. Read the exclusions, not the summary: Israeli policies commonly exclude technical hiking or cap rescue low.
- [ ] Itinerary and hut dates left with someone at home
