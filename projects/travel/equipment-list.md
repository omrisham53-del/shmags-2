# Norway Equipment & Packing List

Everything to pack for the Norway trek (departs Sept 8; 11 hut nights across Jotunheimen + Breheimen, plus the Odda/Trolltunga block). Nothing below is checked off -- owning an item isn't the same as it being in the bag yet. Tags:

- **(NEED TO BUY)** -- not purchased yet
- **(BUY IN NORWAY)** -- deliberately bought locally, not from Israel
- **(CHECK FIRST)** -- verify something before deciding whether to buy

Base advice came from a family member with real hands-on experience in the area; gap-checked and expanded 2026-08-24, purchases tracked as they land. Full backstory and sourcing: `tracker.md` and the decision log.

---

## Sleep System

- [ ] Sleeping bag
- [ ] Sleeping mat
- [ ] Sleeping bag liner (lakenpose) -- mandatory at every DNT cabin, all 11 nights, staffed and self-service. Duvets and pillows aren't washed between guests and can't be used uncovered.

## Footwear

- [ ] Hiking shoes -- test loaded before departure (below-ankle vs. ankle support is an open judgment call given river crossings, boulder fields, and the Besseggen scramble with a full pack)
- [ ] Waterproof pants that cover the top of the shoe (2 pairs)
- [ ] Gaiters **(NEED TO BUY)**

## Clothing

- [ ] Regular t-shirts, 2-3 **(NEED TO BUY)**

- [ ] Thermal base layers
- [ ] Fleece or equivalent mid-layer (thin one)
- [ ] Insulated jacket (Rab)

- [ ] Hat/hood, sun protection for the back of the neck
- [ ] Warm beanie **(CHECK FIRST** -- confirm it's warm enough**)**
- [ ] Buff **(NEED TO BUY)**

Confirm at the Sep 9 DNT visit whether the self-service cabins stock a provision store -- would cut that weight.

## Buy / Sort in Norway (Oslo DNT visit, Sep 9 morning)

Not carried from Israel -- these are picked up locally, most on the Sep 9 morning DNT-center visit.

- [ ] DNT key -- 100 NOK refundable deposit, requires active membership. Breheimen's 4 self-service cabins (Navarsete, Vigdalstøl, Fast, Arentzbu) are locked without it. Collectable/returnable at any DNT staffed lodge.
- [ ] Gas canister -- DNT shop or XXL. Canisters can't fly; confirm the valve matches the stove (standard EN 417 screw-thread) before leaving Israel.
- [ ] Paper map + compass -- also listed under Navigation above
- [ ] Cash -- withdrawn in Norway, not before departure

---

**The 13-week problem:** this list is built for Norway but gets carried through London, Scotland, Ireland and Portugal. Scotland reuses most of it (West Highland Way), so nothing is dead weight until early November -- but the tent, stove and winter layers become baggage for the surf legs. Worth deciding whether anything ships home after Scotland.
