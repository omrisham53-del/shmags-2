# Norway Route -- Final Plan (locked 2026-09-08/09)

Confirmed via DNT Oslo og Omegn's reply (2026-09-04) and Omri's own on-the-ground verification (2026-09-08). Full history/reasoning stays in `tracker.md` and `decisions/log.md` -- this file is just the route to hike from.

## Travel to Jotunheimen

- **Sep 8:** Land Oslo, one night in Oslo.
- **Sep 9:** Full day in Oslo -- DNT center (opens 10:00): collect DNT key (100 NOK deposit), confirm membership active, buy gas canister.
- **Sep 10:** Bus Oslo→Otta→Vågåmo→Gjendesheim, departs 08:30 (verified directly with the operator). Night at Gjendesheim.
