## Email

**To:** info.oslo@dnt.no
**Subject:** Route questions before a solo Jotunheimen + Breheimen trip, Sept 10-21

Hei,

I'm doing a solo hut-to-hut trip through Jotunheimen and then Breheimen, Sept 10-21, and have a
few questions before I finalize the route. I'll also be visiting the Oslo center in person on
Sept 9, so happy to discuss any of this then if that's easier -- but wanted to send it ahead in
case it changes my planning.

**Jotunheimen (Sept 10-15):**
I'm doing the standard 6-day loop from Gjendesheim, but reversed from the usual direction
(Gjendesheim -> Memurubu -> Gjendebu -> Leirvassbu -> Spiterstulen -> Glitterheim ->
Gjendesheim), because Memurubu closes for the season on Sept 13 and this was the only way to
keep every night inside a hut's open season. I understand this puts the Besseggen ridge on Day
1 in its steeper, descending direction, right after arriving with no acclimatization day. Is
this something you'd flag as a real concern for a solo hiker in mid-September, and would you
recommend building in a rest day at Gjendesheim before starting?

**Getting from Gjendesheim to Breheimen (Sept 16):**
I understand the seasonal mountain buses connecting Gjendesheim, Lom and Sogndal have already
stopped running for the year by mid-September. My plan is to take a taxi from Gjendesheim to
Sota Sæter via Lom. Does that sound right, and is there anything about that route or time of
year I should know before booking it?

**Breheimen (Sept 16-21):**
Since Sota Sæter itself closes for the season on Sept 6 and has no self-service option, my plan
is to walk from there to Slæom (about 5 hours) and start the loop from there: Slæom ->
Sprongdalshytta -> Arentzbu -> Fast -> Vigdalstøl -> Fivla -> Navarsete -> Høyheimsvik. A few
specific questions:

1. Slæom to Sprongdalshytta is about 22km, and it would be my first full day after the walk in
   to Slæom the day before. Is that a realistic single day in mid-September, or would you
   suggest splitting it or building in more time?
2. The last day (Vigdalstøl to Høyheimsvik via Fivla and Navarsete) is about 20km across three
   trail sections in one day. Same question -- realistic, or worth splitting?
3. Is Fivla usable as an overnight stop, or is it really just a waypoint shelter along the way?
4. Do you know if Luster Fjordhytter at Høyheimsvik needs to be booked in advance? I haven't
   been able to find contact details for them directly.

**General:**
Any current read on snow or trail conditions for early-to-mid September this year would be very
useful, especially for the Bukkelægeret wire section and Besseggen.

Thanks very much -- I know this is a lot of questions, happy to talk through any of it in person
on the 9th instead if that's more useful for you.

Best,
Omri Shamgar
