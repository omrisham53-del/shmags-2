## How This Updates

After each `/job-tracker` run:
1. New jobs are added to the log with status: **Found**
2. You're asked: "Did you apply to any of these?"
3. Reply with job numbers (e.g., "1, 3, 5") or "none"
4. Statuses update automatically
5. As you hear back from companies, update the Status column manually
