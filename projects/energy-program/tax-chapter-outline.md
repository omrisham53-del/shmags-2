## 4. Methodology

**Status: PARTIALLY BUILT**, asymmetric by design

- **Adoption logic:** the payback-threshold rule (3-year hurdle, Rafi's own number) -- confirmed by Daniel 2026-08-05 as the chapter's strongest conclusion. Real firms use short internal payback hurdles, not a 20-year social NPV; this is what determines whether a technology shows real additionality under the incentive.
- **The additionality finding:** only chillers show genuine additionality at the 3-year threshold; heat pumps and VSD clear the bar with or without the incentive (deadweight). State this plainly -- it's the chapter's real methodological asymmetry vs. the grants chapter, which avoided an additionality claim entirely.
- **Market sizing:** PIVOTED 2026-08-05 -- real per-technology market sizing (chiller construction-based, heat pump/VSD stock-based) didn't reliably answer "how many projects happen per year." Chapter now shows savings per 1,000 units, per technology, as a placeholder, chillers included for consistency, pending Yaniv Giat's reply on import data (sent 2026-08-05, cc Daniel). State this placeholder basis plainly in the methodology text -- don't let a per-1,000-units figure read as a real market-size claim.
- **Fiscal cost:** the tax-shield benefit (C-B, existing model calc) is a timing cost, not a full outlay, unlike the grant -- this is likely the chapter's cost-effectiveness punchline once real numbers are in (cheaper per tCO2 than the grant, if the dual reporting bears that out). Reported per-1,000-units now, same C-B logic just scaled.
- **What's explicitly NOT in the methodology:** the Cyprus-style chillers-only differentiated multiplier was raised and rejected by Daniel (too narrow/arbitrary-looking for a Ministry recommendation) -- don't reintroduce it as a policy recommendation.
