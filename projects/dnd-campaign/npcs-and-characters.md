## Player Characters

### Herald (Eitan) - Human Paladin
**Current Status:** Level 2

**Background:**
- Left an order that suppressed knowledge and restricted freedom
- Spiritually lost, seeking new path and meaning
- Mechanically: Paladin (oath TBD)

**Personal Arc:**
- Discovers his old order deliberately suppressed dragon rider knowledge
- Learns dragon riders' values: freedom + partnership (contrast to order's control)
- Inspired by dragon rider heroism and legacy
- **Goal:** Become a new kind of paladin/spiritual leader based on dragon rider principles

**What He Needs:**
- Discovery of dragon rider history (library scene - TUESDAY)
- Contrast between old order's values and dragon rider values
- Clear inspiration point: "This is what I could become"
- Eventually: Scholar offers him a new oath/belief system

**Notes:**
- Name parallel: "Herald" is also the NPC role (intermediary for Stewards in old lore) - interesting coincidence/foreshadowing potential
- His character name is literally what he needs to become: a herald of a new path

### Aerendil (Raz) - Half-Elf Warlock
**Current Status:** Level 2

**Background:**
- Received warlock powers from unknown entity
- Experiences fiery red dreams
- Doesn't know who or what his patron is

**Personal Arc:**
- Patron is an ANCIENT DRAGON (enslaved by the betrayer)
- Dragon chose him through dreams to help free it
- The fiery red dreams are the dragon reaching out
- **Goal:** Understand his purpose, discover his patron, help free the ancient dragon

**What He Needs:**
- Discover dragon rider fire bloodline lore (library scene hints this)
- Remain mysterious about patron until level 4+ (gradual revelation)
- Personal investment in freeing the ancient dragon
- Eventually: Meet/communicate with the patron dragon directly

**Patron Dragon Details:**
- Incredibly ancient, once very powerful, now weakened by magical binding
- Enslaved by the betrayer (forced into non-consensual bond attempt)
- Has ONE power left: ability to reach humans through dreams
- Chose Aerendil because of potential to help free it
- Gave Aerendil warlock powers for this specific purpose

**Notes:**
- The patron is essentially the emotional heart of the campaign for this character
- His "fiery dreams" are literally the dragon's consciousness reaching out
- This creates deep agency/urgency for the final confrontation

### Bard / Ziggy (Yoav) - Human Bard
**Current Status:** Level 2

**Background:**
- Former band member who thought he was better than the rest
- Left to find his own path
- Found magical instrument at the estate and made deal with Thorn to keep it

**Current Motivation:**
- The magical instrument itself (Harmony Lute)
- Proving himself independent from his old band

**Personal Arc:**
- Discover deeper meaning behind his instrument
- Connect instrument's harmony to ancient dragon rider magic (parallel theme)
- Build group bonds through sharing his backstory
- **Goal:** (Developing) Instrument becomes symbol of his connection to something greater than personal ambition

**What He Needs:**
- Opportunity to share backstory with the group (next session opportunity)
- Discovery that his instrument's harmony mirrors ancient magic
- Group acceptance and integration
- Eventually: Understanding his role in the larger narrative

**The Harmony Lute:**
- Creates perfect harmonic music with spectral band
- Duration: 5 minutes per activation
- Once per long rest
- Mechanical effect: Increases Bardic Inspiration die by one size (d6→d8, d8→d10, etc.)
- No literal connection to dragon riders needed, but thematic parallel
- His use of harmony mirrors how ancient elves bonded with dragons

**Notes:**
- Yoav joined the campaign later (after first sessions), so he's still catching up on lore
- His character's arc is less immediate than the others - more about integration and discovering deeper purpose
- The instrument is his entry point to the larger story

## NPCs

### Thorn - Estate Lord
**Role:** Innocent questgiver, knowledge broker, source of guidance

**Age/Appearance:** 50-60, physically strong but somewhat disheveled

**Personality:**
- History enthusiast but disorganized
- Intelligent, thoughtful, but troubled by his secrets
- Genuinely wants to help but cautious

**Motivations:**
- Influences the noble class through knowledge trading
- Protects his library (which contains forbidden items)
- Fears exposure of his collection

**What He Knows:**
- Dragon riders existed and were powerful
- The knowledge is forbidden (he's uncertain why, suspects it's about fear of resurrection)
- Someone searched his library recently (the druid/betrayer)
- Can point players to the scholar if asked about dragon rider lore

**What He Doesn't Know:**
- The druid's identity
- The specific plan to enslave dragons
- How dangerous the conspiracy actually is

**His Role in Campaign:**
- Session 2: Questgiver, provides context about dragon riders, reveals map discovery
- Ongoing: Can be resource for information, remains neutral/supportive
- Later: Potential witness to betrayer activity, can provide shelter/supplies

**Notes:**
- Keep him innocent - he's a good NPC without being a hero
- His fear of the guards creates interesting constraints (can't call authorities)
- He represents the "ordinary person caught in extraordinary events" archetype

### The Scholar (Female, Name TBD)
**Role:** Mentor figure, source of deep lore, offers new path/oath

**Status:** Exiled from academy, lives in wild

**Background:**
- Scholar researched forbidden dragon rider bloodline material
- Got passionate about their practices and beliefs
- Started creating "weird magic" that mirrors ancient dragon rider magic
- Academy exiled her as a threat

**Personality:**
- Passionate, intellectual, driven by ideas
- Believes in the value of ancient knowledge
- Sympathetic but realistic about dangers

**What She Knows:**
- Dragon rider history, philosophy, and magic
- The betrayer's family connection (descended from dragon rider line)
- Why the research was forbidden
- How to help Herald find his new path
- Deeper mechanics of dragon-human bonding

**What She Can Teach:**
- Herald: New oath/belief system based on dragon rider principles
- Aerendil: Understanding dragon rider fire magic, context for his patron
- Bard: The philosophy behind harmony in dragon bonding
- All: The cost/value of freedom vs. control, the nature of power

**Her Role in Campaign:**
- Session 3+: Primary NPC for levels 3-4
- Provides instruction and guidance
- Becomes emotional anchor for character development
- Potentially at risk (betrayer is hunting her)

**Notes:**
- Her exile is crucial to her character - she's trusted because she's also been wronged by authority
- She represents "knowledge as dangerous but necessary"
- Her bond with players develops through shared understanding of dragon rider legacy

### Burton - Mountain Pathfinder
**Role:** Guide, practical support, comic relief

**Background:**
- Works for a cloth merchant, finding shortcuts and alternative routes through the Hellrock Mountains for transporting goods
- Knows the mountain terrain better than anyone - it's his job
- Introduced by Thorn to guide the players outside the city

**Personality:**
- Floaty, slow voice - clearly a loner who spends most of his time in the mountains
- Collects mushrooms (and uses them - gave some to Ziggy in Session 1, distorted both their perceptions)
- Players don't fully trust him yet - he's odd
- No deeper agenda - genuinely just a skilled guide with mountain knowledge

**What He Knows:**
- The Hellrock Mountains terrain intimately
- Has seen smoke from a camp east that doesn't match trade routes (he'll share this in Session 2)
- Said he doesn't know about any Kobolds in the mountains (truth or lie - left open)

**His Role in Campaign:**
- Practical guide for the mountain exploration arc
- Will eventually lead players toward the Scholar's general area
- Trust builds gradually as he proves useful with terrain

**Notes:**
- Keep his personality consistent: useful, odd, not dangerous
- The Kobold claim (truth or lie) can be resolved later if it becomes relevant

### The Betrayer (Male, Identity TBD)
**Role:** Main antagonist, corrupted dragon rider descendant

**Class/Type:** Druid

**Abilities:**
- Move through nature undetected
- Magic to create dragon-bonding spell/item using blue salt crystals
- Leadership over Kobolds and Darklings
- Descended from dragon rider bloodline - has fire heritage/magic

**Personality:** (To be determined as campaign develops)
- Likely: ambitious, corrupted, sees domination as evolution of dragon riding
- Possible: tragic figure who fell to darkness
- Motivations: unclear until level 4-5 revelation

**His Plan:**
1. Gather blue salt crystals (via Kobold miners under Clerroc)
2. Create spell/magical item for forced dragon bonding
3. Enslave ancient dragon and use it for conquest/domination
4. Resurrect dragon-riding as tool of power, not partnership

**His Current Activity (Levels 1-2):**
- Actively searching library for information (found something)
- Directing Kobolds to mine
- Hunting the scholar (she knows too much about bloodline)
- Organizing Darklings as superior minions

**Hints & Clues (Level 2-5):**
- **Level 2:** Mysterious library searcher, Darklings with Kobolds
- **Level 3-4:** Escalating encounters, organized threat pattern, fire magic parallels
- **Level 4-5:** Identity revealed, family connection to dragon riders becomes clear
- **Level 5-6:** Full plan exposed, final confrontation inevitable

**Notes:**
- Don't reveal too much too fast - mystery builds tension
- The druid element means he's naturally stealthy/connected to nature
- His descent from dragon rider bloodline makes him parallel to Herald (both seeking path/purpose, but opposite directions)

### Ancient Dragon (Aerendil's Patron)

**Role:** Enslaved ally, source of Aerendil's power, emotional core of his arc

**Age/Status:** Ancient, extremely old, powerful once, now weakened by magical binding

**Connection to Aerendil:**
- Reached out through dreams
- Gave him warlock powers
- Chose him specifically to help free it

**Current Status:**
- Enslaved/bound by betrayer
- Betrayer attempting to create permanent non-consensual bond
- One power remaining: ability to communicate through human dreams
- Desperate but not broken

**What Aerendil Knows (So Far):**
- Has a patron (unknown identity)
- Patron communicates through fiery dreams
- Patron gives him red-tinted fire magic
- Nothing else - mystery maintained

**What Aerendil Will Learn:**
- Level 2-3: Patron is a dragon (from lore/dreams)
- Level 4+: Patron was ancient dragon rider
- Level 5+: Patron is enslaved, needs help
- Level 6: Direct communication/aid from patron in final battle

**Notes:**
- The dragon is essentially a character despite being NPC
- The relationship with Aerendil is deeply personal - he's not just a source of power
- His freedom/enslavement is tied directly to the campaign outcome
- Creates emotional weight to the final confrontation
