# Chains
## Adventure 2 – Session 2

---

### Session Goals

- Resolve the combat cliffhanger from Session 1
- Interrogation reveals: Scholar is being hunted, deadline is tomorrow night, religious orders backed her exile
- Players discover the Darkling binding mechanic (crystalline marks)
- Aerendil: Dream 1 - one word, wakes next to fire that wasn't there
- Herald: learns his order was actively complicit in suppressing Dragon Rider knowledge
- Players leave heading toward the Scholar with urgency and a clear deadline
- Burton earns his place as a reliable guide

---

### Scene 1: The Cavern Fight
**Estimated time:** 20-25 minutes

**Setting:**
The cavern mouth. Darklings emerged from the dark after Aerendil's yell, short swords drawn. Kobolds cluster behind them - more scared than aggressive.

**What Happens:**
- Darklings are the real threat - disciplined, dangerous, not easily scared
- Kobolds fold early - one breaks from the group in the first or second round and drops its weapon
- When players get within a few feet of a Darkling, they notice it: faint crystalline marks on the skin, almost like veins of pale blue
- Combat ends with at least one captive (the Kobold that broke, or a Darkling if players subdue one)

**GM Notes:**
- Keep it fast: 2-3 meaningful rounds, not a war of attrition
- The crystalline marks are visible but say nothing about them - let players ask or wonder
- If all enemies are killed with no captive: one Darkling's body has a crude map with a location marked east in the mountains
- The Kobold that broke is genuinely terrified - it will cooperate fast once it believes it can survive

---

### Scene 2: Interrogation
**Estimated time:** 15-20 minutes

**Setting:**
Outside the cavern or just inside, captive restrained. Night, cold mountain air.

**The Kobold (breaks easily):**
Offer it its life in exchange for information. It gives everything without much pressure:
- "She lives east - in the mountains, away from the city. She came from the academy."
- "He wants her alive. She knows the ritual. She knows how the blood works, how to make the bond."
- "We were supposed to move on her tonight. There are more of us - the main group moves tomorrow night."
- "The orders in the city signed off on her exile. The academy didn't act alone."

That last point is for Herald. Let it land quietly - don't underline it or make a scene of it. Watch his player's reaction.

**The Darkling (one piece, under real pressure only):**
The Darkling won't cooperate willingly. Under serious pressure it gives one thing:
- "There are more. The main group moves tomorrow night."

Then stops. Not out of loyalty - the binding costs it when it tries to say more. Players may notice the crystalline marks pulse faintly when it resists.

The Darkling won't give more. It isn't braver than the Kobold - it physically can't. That wrongness should feel like information in itself.

**Character Moment - Herald:**
"The orders in the city signed off on her exile."
He doesn't need to recognize anything specific. He just learns, for the first time, that the institution he came from was active in this suppression. Not passive. Not ignorant. They signed off.
Let him sit with it. Don't rush past it.

### Scene 3: Rest in the Mountains
**Estimated time:** 20-30 minutes

**Setting:**
The group finds a place to rest away from the cavern. Cold mountain air, quiet, open sky.

**Burton:**
When asked about the Scholar's general area, Burton mentions almost offhandedly that he's seen smoke from a camp east that doesn't match any trade route. He assumed it was a hermit. He can take them there.

**Aerendil's Dream 1:**
During watch or early sleep, the dream hits.

> *Heat. Not painful - the opposite. A warmth that comes from somewhere far below everything, pressing upward. A presence at the edge of it, enormous and old and very far away. Then one word, not quite heard but felt:*
> **"Chains."**
> *He wakes up. There is a small fire beside him. There was no fire before.*

No option to continue yet. This is Dream 1 - just the opening. He knows it was real. He knows it connects to the crystal and the fire from the night before.

The fire is small, warm, non-threatening. No explanation. It just is.

**Group Moment:**
The group has time to process what they learned. Herald's player may want to sit with what the Kobold said about the orders. Ziggy can push conversations if his player wants. Burton is present but doesn't dominate - he's waiting for them to be ready to move.

**GM Notes:**
- Don't rush this scene - it's where character moments breathe
- Let players lead the conversation
- The deadline should stay present: they need to move before tomorrow night

---

### Scene 4: The Journey East
**Estimated time:** 30-40 minutes

**Setting:**
Mountain terrain. Burton leads east through paths no one else would know. Steep, rocky, quiet except for wind.

**What Happens:**
- Burton navigates confidently - this is his terrain. Players start to trust him practically even if they still find him odd.
- At some point Burton stops and looks back at the group. He says there's a faster way - a lower tunnel system that cuts an hour or two off the journey. He's avoided it because something lives down there. He doesn't know what exactly. But if getting there first matters, it might be worth the risk. Players choose.
- At some point they pass ruins of an old shrine. It predates the current order by centuries. The imagery carved into the stone shows humans alongside creatures - not commanding them, alongside them. Partners. Nobody explains it. Burton doesn't know what it is. Herald sees it.
- End the session either arriving within sight of a small camp with smoke rising from it (the Scholar's), or finding fresh signs of Darkling activity - recent tracks, a dropped weapon, disturbed ground - close to where the Scholar is.

Either ending creates the same hook for Session 3: she's real, she's close, and they're not the only ones looking.

**Optional Encounter: The Carrion Crawler**

*Only if players take the shortcut.*

A low tunnel system cutting through the mountain. Narrow, damp, ceiling close enough to feel claustrophobic. The walls are slick. It smells wrong - decay and something chemical. Burton moves through quietly, less confident than usual.

The Carrion Crawler drops from the ceiling when the party is mid-tunnel, no good exit nearby. It goes for the nearest target with its tentacles first. The real threat isn't damage - it's the paralysis.

*Mechanic: Tentacle hit forces DC 13 CON save or target is poisoned and paralyzed for up to 1 minute. A paralyzed character at level 2 with enemies nearby is a serious problem - allies have to choose between fighting the crawler and protecting the downed player.*

The encounter shouldn't kill anyone, but it should cost them. Spell slots burned, HP drained, maybe one character needed to be dragged through the last stretch. They arrive faster but in worse shape.

**If they skip the shortcut:**
They take the safe path. They arrive on time - but just barely. Fresh Darkling tracks very close to the Scholar's camp. The main squad hasn't moved on her yet, but it's clear they're near.

**GM Notes:**
- The shrine is a visual beat, not a lecture. No explanation given - Herald's player will understand.
- Keep Burton's dialogue practical and slightly odd - useful but still strange
- The deadline matters: Burton can reference it ("we need to move if we want to arrive before they do")
- Don't spring the Carrion Crawler without the choice first - the player decision is the point
- Burton is visibly rattled after the crawler encounter - this wasn't part of his plan
- Both paths (shortcut or safe) lead to the same session ending hook, just with different resource states

### Reference: The Darkling Binding

The Betrayer has been using blue salt crystal magic to bind the Darklings to him - a forced, non-consensual magical connection. The same principle he intends to apply to the ancient dragon, tested first on smaller creatures.

**Visual:** Faint crystalline marks on the Darkling's skin, like pale blue veins. More visible when it tries to resist or betray.

**Effect:** Darklings cannot betray the Betrayer without the binding physically resisting. The cost increases the more they fight it.

**What Players Know Right Now:** Something is wrong with the Darklings. The marks are there. The explanation comes later.

**What This Means Thematically:** The Darklings aren't just loyal soldiers - they're victims of the same magic threatening the dragon. This reframes them without announcing it.

---

### Reference: Aerendil's Dream System

**Dream 1 (this session):**
Heat, presence, one word ("chains"), wakes next to a fire that wasn't there. No option to continue. Just the opening.

**Dream 2 onward:**
After each dream, Aerendil is offered the option to go back in. Each time he continues, he wakes up closer to the fire.

**Final dream:**
He goes fully into the fire. The patron reveals itself.

**Clarity progression:**
Dreams start as sensations and single words. As the campaign progresses they build into images, then fragments of meaning, then something closer to direct contact.

- The Scholar is real, east in the mountains, and in immediate danger

- Something is wrong with the Darklings (the marks)
- Aerendil felt something real in the dream - and woke next to a fire that wasn't there
- Herald's order wasn't passive - they signed off on the Scholar's exile
- Burton knows this terrain and he's useful
