Lyre of Harmonic Resonance
Wondrous Instrument
Uncommon
Description
When played, this enchanted lyre summons spectral musicians (violin, drums, bass, and flute) who play in perfect harmony with the wielder. The music creates a compelling atmosphere and bolsters the spirits of allies.
Activation
  • Action: Bonus action
  • Frequency: Once per long rest
  • Duration: 5 minutes or until dismissed
Magical Effects
  • Bardic Inspiration dice increase by one size
  • Allies within 30 feet gain advantage on Wisdom saves
  • Music is audible but not excessively loud
  • Spectral musicians play what the wielder desires
