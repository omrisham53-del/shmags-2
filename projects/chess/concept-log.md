# Concept Log

One entry per studied chapter of Rozman's *How to Win at Chess: Intermediate*, in Omri's own words. Read the book in order: it is a sequenced curriculum and skipping around breaks concepts that depend on earlier ones.

Each entry names the `patterns.py` pattern that back-searches the archive for it, so the concept gets tested against his own games rather than just read. Entries without a pattern are concepts the matcher cannot express yet.
