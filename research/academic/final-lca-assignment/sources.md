## Secondary sources (industry/market context, section 1.2–1.3)

4. Global Cement and Concrete Association (GCCA). *2050 Cement and Concrete Industry Net Zero Roadmap*. https://gccassociation.org/concretefuture/ — cement production accounts for ~7% of global CO2 emissions; 40 leading manufacturers committed to a further 25% cut by 2030 under a seven-point decarbonization plan (reduced clinker factor, fuel switching, CCUS). Retrieved 2026-07-21.
   Note: Interbeton's own EPD is built on the GCCA Industry EPD Tool for Cement and Concrete and explicitly cites this same roadmap and GCCA's climate ambition — direct link between the roadmap and the primary source EPD.

5. Olsson, J. A., Miller, S. A., & Kneifel, J. D. (2024). A review of current practice for life cycle assessment of cement and concrete. *Resources, Conservation and Recycling*, 206, Article 107619. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0921344924002131 — identifies inconsistent system-boundary selection as a primary source of incomparability across cement/concrete LCAs, and finds clinker/kiln-stage (A1) emissions as the dominant climate-change contributor across studies. Retrieved 2026-07-21.
