# HW #2: LCA of Dairy vs. Soy Milk - Research Notes

## Key Findings

### Water Footprint
- **Dairy milk:** 628 liters of water per liter of milk produced
- **Soy milk:** 27-28 liters per liter produced
- **Difference:** Soy milk uses ~22 times less water than dairy
- **Data verified against:** Poore & Nemecek (2018)

### Environmental Hotspots
- **Dairy milk:** Raw milk production is the primary hotspot (75-85% of impacts)
  - Drivers: feed production, fertilizer use, enteric fermentation, on-site emissions
- **Soy milk:** Processing, packaging, transportation are hotspots (not raw material production)
- **Data verified against:** González-García et al. (2025) - systematic review of 84 LCA studies

### Processing Details

**Dairy milk:**
- Pasteurization: heating to kill pathogens
- Cooling for freshness maintenance
- Energy-intensive due to continuous refrigeration

**Soy milk:**
- Soaking dried soybeans
- Mechanical grinding into slurry
- Boiling 15-20 minutes to inactivate trypsin inhibitor (STI)
- Straining and filtration to remove soy pulp
- Defoaming agents (glycerin-based fatty acid esters) added during boiling
- **Data verified against:** Cao et al. (2009) on trypsin inhibitor inactivation

### Lifecycle Stages (6 total)

1. **Raw Material Extraction** - Feed/soybeans, water, energy
2. **Material Processing & Manufacturing** - Pasteurization/boiling, cooling
3. **Assembly, Packaging & Preparation** - Carton assembly (paper, plastic, aluminum)
4. **Distribution & Retail Delivery** - Cold chain logistics, retail refrigeration
5. **Use Phase** - Home storage in refrigerators (2-3 weeks shelf life)
6. **End-of-Life** - Carton recycling, wastewater treatment

### Functional Unit
One carton (1 liter) of refrigerated milk delivered to consumer - allows fair comparison between systems serving same purpose
