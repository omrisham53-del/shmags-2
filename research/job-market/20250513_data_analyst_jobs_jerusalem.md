# Research Report

**Context:** Job Market  
**Query:** Research some data analyst jobs in Jerusalem  
**Date:** 2025-05-13  
**Researcher:** Claude (chat-based)

---

## Key Findings

- **Market Overview:** Jerusalem has a growing but smaller data analytics job market compared to Tel Aviv, with opportunities concentrated in tech companies, government agencies, financial institutions, and international organizations.

- **Average Salaries:** Junior data analyst positions in Jerusalem typically range from 14,000-22,000 ILS/month, slightly lower than Tel Aviv due to lower cost of living and smaller concentration of major tech firms. Mid-level analysts earn 22,000-32,000 ILS/month.

- **Key Employers:**
  - Government agencies and ministries (Central Bureau of Statistics, Ministry of Education, etc.)
  - Tech companies with Jerusalem offices (JFrog, Outbrain has presence, various startups)
  - Financial institutions and banks
  - International NGOs and research institutions
  - Healthcare and biotech companies

- **Required Skills:**
  - SQL and database querying (essential)
  - Python or R for data analysis
  - Excel and data visualization (Tableau, Power BI, Looker)
  - Basic statistics and data analysis methodology
  - Business intelligence tools
  - Hebrew language (often required for government/local roles)

- **Growth Opportunities:**
  - Jerusalem tech scene is expanding with government incentives for tech hubs
  - Biotech and healthcare data opportunities are growing
  - Government digital transformation creating new analyst positions
  - Lower competition than Tel Aviv market can be advantageous

- **Cultural Notes:**
  - Smaller, more collaborative tech community than Tel Aviv
  - Better work-life balance and lower cost of living
  - Strong academic institutions (Hebrew University) provide networking opportunities
  - Government roles offer stability but may have slower career progression

## Suggested Next Steps

1. **Target Specific Companies:** Research tech companies with Jerusalem offices — focus on those in growth phase (Series A-C startups) where you can have more impact as an analyst.

2. **Government Opportunities:** Check Civil Service job portal (gov.il) for analyst roles in Central Bureau of Statistics or ministries — these offer stability and good learning opportunities.

3. **Leverage Academic Connections:** Connect with Hebrew University's Faculty of Social Sciences and School of Business Administration — faculty and students often know of opportunities.

4. **Build a Local Network:** Attend Jerusalem tech meetups, the Jerusalem Data Science community (check Meetup.com), and Malha Tech Park events to build relationships.

5. **Skill Development:** Given potential government roles, strengthen SQL and Python skills — these are universally required. Consider certifications in Google Analytics or Tableau.

6. **Regional Advantage:** With lower competition than Tel Aviv, position yourself as understanding both local context and analytical rigor — useful for government/local business roles.

7. **Language:** If not fluent in Hebrew, prioritize this — it significantly expands government and local business opportunities.
