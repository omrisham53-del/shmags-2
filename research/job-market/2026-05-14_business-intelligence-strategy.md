## 2. SKILLS VALUED

### **Technical Skills** (Non-Negotiable)
| Skill | Importance | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| **SQL** | Critical | Required for 95%+ of roles |
| **Python/R** | Critical | Preferred for advanced analytics |
| **Tableau/Power BI** | Critical | Dashboard & visualization |
| **Google Analytics/Mixpanel** | High | Event tracking & product analytics |
| **Excel** (Advanced) | High | Still heavily used in Israel |
| **Database management** | High | PostgreSQL, MySQL, data warehousing |
| **ETL Tools** | High | Talend, Informatica, or cloud equivalents |
| **Cloud platforms** | High | GCP, AWS, Azure (increasingly required) |
| **Statistics/A/B Testing** | Medium-High | For strategy roles |

### **Business & Soft Skills** (Critical for Strategy Track)
- **Strategic thinking** - Ability to translate data into business recommendations
- **Communication** - Presenting findings to non-technical stakeholders (Hebrew + English)
- **Economics background** - *Your advantage* - understanding market dynamics, pricing strategy
- **Storytelling with data** - Moving beyond reports to actionable narratives
- **Business acumen** - Understanding P&L, KPIs, business models
- **Cross-functional collaboration** - Working with product, marketing, sales teams

### **Domain-Specific** (Preferred, Not Always Required)
- Marketing analytics (attribution, CAC, LTV)
- Financial modeling
- Supply chain optimization
- Healthcare/biotech domain knowledge
