## Locked decisions (detail)

### Reveal ceiling (hold this everywhere)
They MAY conclude: the binding is dragon-rider bonding magic twisted into forced control, and someone with real dragon-rider knowledge is behind the Darklings. They must NOT yet learn: who he is (Ashar, ancient, presumed dead), or that there is a real dragon he is actually after. The Darklings reading as "practice for something bigger" stays a feeling, not a stated fact. (Reveal order: Aerendil's patron via dreams -> Ashar as Betrayer (L4-5) -> truth of the Fall (last).)

### Interrogation
- The **Kobold breaks easily** and is the real information source (under the ceiling): someone wants the Scholar alive because she knows the ritual / "how the blood works"; there's a deadline; the orders backed her exile (Herald's gut-punch).
- The **Darkling physically can't talk** — the binding resists, and the harder the party pushes, the more it costs the Darkling, until **pushing too hard is what kills it**. The horror is caused by the players' own pressure. This makes the Darkling the source of dread (not info) and quietly teaches them what forced bonding does to a living thing.

### Aerendil's Dream 1 (during the rest)
Fires as in the existing dream system (one word, wakes next to a fire that wasn't there). His **player decides** whether to share it.

### The journey: beats distributed across the two paths
- **Shortcut (dangerous, faster):** holds the **Carrion Crawler** danger AND the **Burton-under-pressure** moment (the dangerous tunnel tests who he is).
- **Safe path (slower):** holds the **Darkling squad signs** (fresh tracks, dropped blade near the Scholar's camp) — the "safe" route is the one where they *see* they're in a race.
- **The remembering-shrine:** they pass it **either way** — the fixed heartbeat of the trip.

### The shrine and Herald (his big beat this session)
- Register chosen: a **feeling**, not a vision or memory. The shrine doesn't haunt him, it *remembers*. If his player leans in (kneels, touches it, tries a rite), he gets a breath of what *freely chosen* devotion felt like — the real thing his hollow temple rituals were only a copy of. The vision *shows*; the wanting is *his* response. Intensity scales with how far the player leans in.
- Reframes his arc from "spiritually lost" to "I've been kneeling at a hollow copy of something that was once real." Aims him at *wanting the alternative* by session's end (his target emotional state), which is the on-ramp to the Oath at the Scholar.
- Keep it a **different texture from Aerendil's dreams** (Aerendil = a patron reaching in; Herald = something in him / the place waking up). Both land in the same stretch, so the contrast keeps each special.
- The feeling leaves a **physical, perceptual residue**: this is the seed ("The Stir") of Herald's bond-and-binding sense system (below).

### Herald's bond-and-binding sense — mini-system (4-rung ladder)
His spine, the way the dreams are Aerendil's, but a sense he *wields* (active, sacred), not a passive intrusion. Aimed narrowly at **free vs chained / forced-binding magic**, tied to the campaign's central magic. Build the full thing when his player picks the Oath; for now only "The Stir" exists.
- **L2 (the shrine, now) — The Stir:** pure narrative, no dice. Near the bound Darklings he feels a wrongness he can't name (cold ache / pressure / "something here is chained"). Can't locate or read it. The door opening.
- **L3 (Oath at the Scholar) — crystallizes, Oath-flavored:** a usable sense; steady himself and feel whether anything near is held by forced magic, roughly where and how strongly. Can't break it, only perceive it. His Oath colors *how it reads* (Ancients = a wound in nature; Redemption = the victim inside the chain; Devotion = a broken vow; Vengeance = the hand that made it).
- **L4-5 — Read and ease:** matures from detect to understand (how a bond was made, what it costs the victim, whether it can be undone); can briefly *ease* a bound creature's pain (flavored off Lay on Hands); can feel something vast and chained far off (foreshadows the dragon, unnamed).
- **L6 (climax) — Touch the chain:** becomes a lever. He feels the dragon's failing binding, finds the decaying seam, and is the one who perceives how to loosen it. Doesn't solo-break it, but the party's path to freeing the dragon runs through *his* sense. His arc cashes out as a mechanical role in the finale.
- **Guardrails:** perception/utility/roleplay, NOT damage (no bonus smite). Lean on resources he already has (a Divine Sense use, a Channel Divinity at L3) so it has a cost and cap. Keep numbers loose. Keep the sense NARROW (binding/forced magic only) so it doesn't vacuum mystery out of scenes.
- When finalized: graduate this into `npcs-and-characters.md` (Herald) or a mechanics file, and log in `decisions/log.md`.

### The Carrion Crawler (shortcut danger)
- **One only.** CR 2, nasty +8 tentacle, DC 13 CON paralysis. For three level-2 PCs the threat is the **paralysis cascade** (one down, the other two must choose between killing it and covering the friend). A second would tip to TPK.
- **Drag-not-kill safety valve:** play it true to type — it paralyzes prey to *drag off and eat later*, not to finish on the spot. A paralyzed PC gets hauled toward its nook; the scene becomes a rescue. They arrive wrecked but alive = the "really weakened, under pressure" feel without a funeral.
- **Informed choice:** Burton flags that something lives down there before they commit. Shortcut buys time but costs blood; safe path costs time but stays whole.

### The Scholar = milestone, level 3
Reaching her levels them to 3; the Oath opens session 3.

**Baseline takeaway (everyone, even the 5-minute version):** she was exiled for reconstructing the riders' bonding ritual; the crystal-binding on the Darklings is that ritual perverted (willing partnership -> forced leash); whoever's behind it has real dragon-rider knowledge.

**Early-arrival bonus (reward for the bloody shortcut, ~30-60 min). Choose 1-3 (OPEN, see below):**
- **The original truth:** the bond was never meant to control — blue salt *calmed* dragons so a bond could be *offered and accepted*, both ways. Hearing what it was supposed to be makes the forced version obscene. Lands hardest on Herald + the freedom/control spine.
- **Why they need her specifically:** the ritual is incomplete without a piece only she reconstructed — that's why she's hunted *alive*. Personalizes the deadline; she's the linchpin to protect.
- **The "too big for Darklings" thread:** she notes, almost to herself, this magic was never built for small creatures — they're rehearsals. Stops short of saying for *what*; players' stomachs drop on their own.

**Scholar personality (draft, name TBD):**
- Core: not a kindly mentor. An obsessive truth-keeper who lost career, home, and safety for knowledge nobody wanted; hunted long enough to trust no one. Under the wariness, a fierce, grieving idealism about the partnership age the world buried.
- Manner: tests them before giving anything; precise, a little prickly, corrects their history. But when she talks about the *old riders* the armor cracks and the idealist shows — that crack is what reaches Herald.
- Texture: when they arrive she's mid-decision about whether to run, papers half-packed, guarding a reconstructed diagram. Asks them questions as much as she answers (hunters or help?).
- The clock changes her: 5 minutes = already half-fled, jumpy, firing the compressed version while she grabs her work. An hour = room to test them, thaw, and actually teach (where the real history and the Herald pull come through).
- Name options floated (Omri to pick / veto): **Maren, Sera, Vesna, Ildove** (or something elvish-academic).
