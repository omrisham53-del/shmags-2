## Key source: Government Decision 1261 (14.01.2024) — full text provided by Omri
"תמחור פליטות מזהמים מקומיים וגזי חממה" (Pricing of local pollutant emissions and GHGs). 37th government. Cancels prior Decision 286 (1.8.2021). Follows Decision 171 (25.7.2021) on transition to a low-carbon economy. Rationale stated: correct the market failure where polluters don't pay for environmental damage; pricing emissions is the most efficient regulation to cut them.

**The carbon tax (Section 1):** amends the fuel excise (בלו) + purchase-tax orders to gradually internalize the external cost of carbon + local pollutants. Escalating ₪/ton schedule 2025 -> 2030+:
- Coal (פחם): 147 / 195 / 255 / 335 / 415 / 515
- Natural gas (גז טבעי): 33 / 54 / 80 / 114 / 149 / 192
- Fuel oil 0.5% (מזוט): 92 / 204 / 344 / 531 / 717 / 951
- Fuel oil 1%: 1,168 / 1,281 / 1,421 / 1,607 / 1,794 / 2,028
- LPG (גפ"מ): 176 / 239 / 318 / 423 / 527 / 658
- Petcoke (פטקוק): 110 / 198 / 307 / 454 / 600 / 783
- To be published as a direct carbon tax (₪/ton carbon).

**Complementary support for industry (Section 2) — THIS is where Omri's grant chapter lives:**
- 2a: ministries (Finance, Energy, Economy, Env. Protection) set a budget-support procedure for fuel-consuming plants, **mimicking the compensation they'd get under the EU ETS** free-allocation, preserving efficiency incentives; extra protection for **trade-exposed industries** (carbon-leakage); baseline quota from actual normalized last-known data; declining emission targets; procedure within 6 months; if the procedure isn't published by Dec 31, the tax hike is delayed.
- **2b: if the carbon-tax order takes effect, set a procedure to assist the efficiency upgrades needed to adapt to the carbon tax — UP TO ₪450 MILLION over 2024-2027.** If the tax doesn't start / doesn't rise per schedule in a year, that year's grant is pushed to the next year. *(This 450M / 2024-2027 envelope is almost certainly the "new rounds" the chapter analyzes.)*
- 2c: accelerate natural-gas grid rollout (Gas Authority) to cut use of fuel oil / LPG.
- Section 3: Env. Protection to tap the Cleanliness Fund to support fuel-to-waste conversion.
- Section 4: household electricity price guard — if avg price rises above CPI due to fuel/interest/FX, suspend/reduce the natural-gas excise hike for a year.
- Section 5: Finance to publish aid for weak populations facing energy price rises.
- Section 6: if the carbon-tax escalation is delayed/cancelled, Sections 2-5 are cancelled/suspended accordingly.

Confirms the framing: the grants are explicitly a **"צעד משלים"** (complementary measure) to the carbon tax, to help industry with efficiency + the transition to alternative fuels and create business certainty for the required investments.

## Potential gaps to pressure-test before Omer (Omri's "am I missing something?")
1. **Legal conditionality of the whole program (Sections 2b + 6).** The ₪450M grants flow only "if the carbon-tax order takes effect," and if the tax doesn't start / doesn't rise on schedule in a given year, that year's grant defers to the next year. Section 6: if the tax escalation is cancelled or delayed, Sections 2-5 (including the grants) are cancelled/suspended. The chapter's forecast of rounds running 2025-2027 is therefore CONTINGENT on the tax being implemented and escalating. -> needs an explicit caveat / downside scenario, not a single deterministic forecast.
2. **"Preserve efficiency incentives" framing (2a wording, applies to intent).** The decision explicitly requires support that PRESERVES incentives to improve efficiency. So the section should frame the grants as ACCELERATING the transition (rewarding real efficiency/switching investment), not merely "softening the blow" of the tax. A pure-compensation framing would contradict the regulation's stated intent. Sharpens the "carrot paired with stick" message.
3. **Natural gas is ALSO taxed (and the gas tax rises 33 -> 192).** New rounds support switching to electric OR natural gas, but gas is taxed too (just less than fuel oil/coal) and 2c accelerates the gas grid. So fuel-oil -> gas switching captures a PARTIAL, shrinking benefit as the gas tax climbs, and Section 4 can even suspend the gas-tax hike to protect electricity prices. Gas-switch project economics are less clean than electric-switch — worth acknowledging.
4. **Boundary with Section 2a (EU-ETS-style compensation).** 2a is a separate, output-based compensation mechanism with carbon-leakage protection for trade-exposed industry. The chapter should state it covers 2b (capex efficiency grants) only, so a reader doesn't conflate the two or double-count support to the same plants.
5. **Budget reconciliation.** Confirm the analyzed rounds' total budget reconciles to the "up to ₪450M, 2024-2027" cap (Omri's rounds are 2025-2027; the cap window opens 2024).
6. **Optional strengthener.** Because the analysis assumes no additionality, the escalating tax schedule (Section 1) is a natural way to SHOW why grants now accelerate what the tax will eventually force — connects the two instruments and reinforces the "carrot + stick" story without claiming causal additionality.
