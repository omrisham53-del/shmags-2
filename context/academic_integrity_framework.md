# Academic Integrity & Research Framework

**Updated:** May 16, 2026  
**Based on:** HW #2 Functional Unit and System Boundary assignment

## What This Is

A systematic framework for approaching university coursework with academic rigor: proper sourcing, citation, documentation, and AI transparency.

## Key Principles

1. **Source before finalizing** - Never submit specific claims (numbers, processes, facts) without peer-reviewed support
2. **Cite as you write** - Build bibliography simultaneously, not after
3. **Verify data** - Compare your initial estimates/knowledge against published research
4. **Document methodology** - Show how you sourced, verified, and structured your work
5. **Disclose AI use** - Be transparent about what AI assisted with vs. your original thinking

## Process Example: HW #2

**Assignment:** Life Cycle Assessment of dairy vs. soy milk
**Duration:** One session
**Outcome:** Complete academic assignment with proper sourcing, citations, and AI disclosure

**Process:**
1. Initial draft with best estimates
2. Identified 3 major claims needing sources: water usage, processing methods, environmental hotspots
3. Searched Google Scholar, MDPI, ScienceDirect for peer-reviewed LCA studies
4. Found 4 primary sources (Poore & Nemecek 2018, Cao et al. 2009, Geburt et al. 2022, González-García et al. 2025)
5. Verified data against published research (corrected water usage from 6-8L to 628L)
6. Integrated in-text citations throughout
7. Built bibliography in APA format with DOI links
8. Created AI disclosure explaining assistance provided
9. Created research documentation in research/academic/ folder

## Why This Matters

- **Academic credibility:** Sourced work gets better grades and trust
- **Learning efficiency:** Proper research prevents major revisions
- **Professional growth:** Building research skills for post-university work (policy analysis, data-driven roles)
- **Integrity:** Transparent disclosure of AI use shows you understand ethical collaboration

## For Future Assignments

**Before starting:**
1. Read RESEARCH_GUIDELINES.md
2. Create assignment subfolder in research/academic/
3. Plan research before writing

**While writing:**
4. Document sources in sources.md as you find them
5. Verify data against peer-reviewed research
6. Add in-text citations as you write
7. Keep bibliography synced

**Before submitting:**
8. Review citations for accuracy
9. Create AI disclosure if applicable (use template)
10. Verify bibliography completeness
