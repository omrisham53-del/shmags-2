# Session 1 Notes: T'horn's Estate Library
## Adventure 2 – Session 1 | Played: May 19, 2026

---

### What Happened

**In the Library (Thorn's Estate):**
- Players discovered Dragon Rider history through books, paintings, and handwritten notes
- Magic items distributed: Cloak of Elvenkind → Aerendil, Lyre of Harmonic Resonance → Ziggy
- Saw illustrations of the Dragon Stand at Darkhollow (heroic tale) and the Fall of House Arendath (cautionary tale)
- Got hints about the connection between dragons and dragon riders
- Thorn was very worried - players told him "this is a big deal, we can't stay quiet about this"
- This led to the decision: explore outside the city, specifically the river entrance to the underground area beneath Kell Rock

**Thorn gave them Burton** as a guide for the mission outside the city

**Ziggy investigated further in the library:**
- Learned Dragon Rider knowledge is forbidden because the kingdom fears a new rise of dragon rider power
- Found books about the bloodline and the estate's previous owners
- Improvised estate backstory: the estate was built by humans with a deep connection to the elves/dragon riders, with cooling pipe systems to preserve Dragon Rider artifacts, scrolls, and books - to keep their legacy alive

**In Town:**
- Herald went to the temple in Kell Rock - his first visit. Tried old rituals he hadn't performed in a long time. It felt familiar but also hollow. A young monk spoke with him briefly and told him about Gali (the friendly head monk); Gali himself did not approach. Herald ran.
- Aerendil asked people around town about the blue crystals - risky move. More people now know they exist. Could blow back on Thorn if word reaches the wrong ears.
- Small visit to a blacksmith - tried to determine if the crystals were metal. No one knows. Common folk have no knowledge of the crystals or their connection to dragon bonding.

**Night Watch - Aerendil's Crystal Moment:**
During his watch, Aerendil took one of the blue salt crystals from Herald's satchel (without asking). He put it in the fire. Felt it was right instinctively. Put his hands into the fire with the crystal - felt his body fill with energy, was not burned. Stopped when he heard a noise from the direction of the cavern. Returned the crystals to Herald's satchel. Herald does not know this happened.

**Meeting Burton:**
- Met at the town exit
- Weird character - floaty slow voice, collects mushrooms
- Burton and Ziggy both took mushrooms that distorted their perception of reality
- Burton said he doesn't know about any Kobolds in the mountains (truth or lie - left open)

**The Cavern:**
- Burton led them up the hill east toward Kell Rock Mountains
- Ziggy was first to spot light coming from a small cavern in the mountain
- Players decided to send Ziggy to stealth in while the others made noise (counterproductive - creatures inside heard them)
- Darklings and Kobolds were hiding inside the cavern, scared (they know the players are powerful from the Kobold mine fight)
- Ziggy stealth-checked in, saw no one (they were hidden)
- Aerendil yelled into the cavern
- Darklings revealed themselves with short swords drawn

**Session ends at the start of combat**
