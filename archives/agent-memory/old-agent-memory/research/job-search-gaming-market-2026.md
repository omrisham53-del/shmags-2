# Gaming Industry Data Analyst Market in Israel (May 2026)

## Market Sizing

The Israeli gaming job market for junior data analysts is **stable but selective** (not explosive growth). Market is in recovery phase post-2023-2024 slowdown. Entry-level positions: ILS 95,000-130,000 annually (most common: ILS 110,000-120,000).

## Primary Target Companies

**Tier 1 (High Priority):**
- **Playtika** (Herzliya) - Largest, most active hiring, clear career path, best benefits
- **Scopely** (Tel Aviv) - Consistent hiring, good internship-to-hire pipeline
- **888 Holdings** (Ramat Hasharon) - Growing analytics team, moderate hiring activity

**Tier 2 (Fallback):**
- **Playkot** (Jerusalem), **TabTale** (Tel Aviv), emerging gaming startups

**Why:** These three are the safest bets. Playtika especially — continuous hiring, enterprise-level training, best salary + benefits.

## Critical Skills Needed

**Must-Have (Non-Negotiable):**
1. **SQL** - Multi-table joins, subqueries, CTEs. Single biggest gap if weak. Learn it first (3-4 weeks).
2. **Python** (secondary: R) - ETL, modeling, analysis. 6-8 weeks to competency after SQL.
3. **Excel** - Pivot tables, VLOOKUP, VBA. Omri's strength from EcoTraders; differentiator.
4. **Gaming domain knowledge** - F2P economics, LTV/CAC/ARPU, retention cohorts, player metrics. Self-taught in 2-3 weeks.

**Nice-to-Have:**
- BI tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- Google Analytics
- A/B testing methodology

## Omri's Positioning

**Strengths:**
- Excel mastery (EcoTraders), analytical rigor (economics), communication skills (policy writing)
- Unique angle: analyst with policy credibility, not typical "data nerd"
- Can command upper-mid salary range (ILS 120,000-130,000) with strong portfolio

**Gaps:**
- SQL (must learn immediately)
- Python/R (learn after SQL)
- Gaming domain knowledge (learnable, falls into place quickly)

**Realistic Timeline:** 8-12 weeks from now (May) to offer by August 2026 for October start.

- Playtika and Scopely are safest bets; smaller studios less predictable
