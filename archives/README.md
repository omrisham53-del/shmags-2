# Archives

Completed projects, old session summaries, and historical work.

## What Goes Here

- Completed projects (moved from `projects/`)
- Session summaries older than current quarter
- Old research that's no longer relevant
- Historical notes and decisions

## Organization

- `projects/` - Completed project folders
- `sessions/` - Old session summaries
- `research/` - Archived research by topic

## When to Archive

Move items here when:
- Project is 100% complete and no longer needs active tracking
- Session is older than 30 days and no longer frequently referenced
- Research is historical and not actively informing current decisions

Use archives to keep the active workspace clean while preserving work history.
