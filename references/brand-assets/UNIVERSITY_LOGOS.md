# Reichman University Logos

Guide for obtaining official Reichman University (IDC Herzliya) logos.

## Official Sources

### Primary: Reichman University Official Site
- Main site: [runi.ac.il](https://www.runi.ac.il/en/)
- Economics School: [Tiomkin Economics School](https://www.runi.ac.il/en/schools/economics/)
- Contact communications department for brand assets

### Secondary: Campus.gov.il (Israeli Higher Education)
- [Reichman University on campus.gov.il](https://campus.gov.il/en/institution/reichman-university/)

## What You Need

1. **Main University Logo** - Reichman University (new branding, 2021+)
   - For university-wide assignments
   
2. **School Logos** - Tiomkin Economics School / Sustainability programs
   - Department-specific assignments
   - More targeted branding

3. **Formats to Request**
   - **PNG** (for digital documents, presentations)
   - **SVG** (for scalability in web/print)
   - **High-res versions** (300dpi+ for printing)

## How to Get Them

### Option 1: Download from Official Branding Page
Check the Reichman official website for a "Brand Assets" or "Press Kit" page where logos may be available for download.

### Option 2: Contact Communications
Email or call Reichman's communications/marketing department:
- They typically provide logos for students and affiliates
- Request: "Student use on academic assignments"
- Ask for: PNG and SVG formats, both light and dark backgrounds

### Option 3: Check Your Student Portal
Your university portal may have downloadable brand assets for authorized use on academic work.

## Logo Info
The Reichman logo includes seven stars representing Zionism, leadership, and innovation (from Benjamin Herzl's flag suggestion).

## Save Location
Once downloaded, organize by format:
```
brand-assets/
  university-logos/
    reichman-main-logo.png
    tiomkin-economics-logo.png
    sustainability-school-logo.png
    [SVG versions as needed]
```

## Storage Tips
- Keep originals in brand-assets
- Create scaled/cropped versions in specific project folders as needed
- Document the source and date obtained
